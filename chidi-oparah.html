Summary

You've raised the capital. You have the customers. But your architecture, your delivery, and your governance aren't keeping pace and your board is starting to notice. You now have multiple teams, products, and initiatives running at once. Priorities collide. Decisions keep bouncing back to you. Technical debt compounds. Your best people are stretched.

This is the moment where effort stops being the answer. What you need now is structure.

I'm Chidi Oparah, Founder, Fractional CTO, Enterprise Architect, SAFe SPC, and Transformation Coach. I have 25+ years inside JPMorgan, Mastercard/Vocalink, HSBC, Nokia, and Vodafone. I've seen what scaling chaos looks like from the inside and I know exactly what it takes to replace it with structural clarity and executable momentum.

I work with technology-led organisations, typically 60 to 120 engineers, scaling fast and under board or investor pressure to deliver, through my proprietary 6E Method™: Evaluate, Envision, Empower, Equip, Enable, Evolve.

I work embedded, not detached. Steering forums, architecture reviews, leadership sessions. Not reports from the outside. All engagements are fixed-fee and anchored to outcomes. Never day rates. I use AI-assisted analysis and diagnostic tooling as part of my working method for faster synthesis, sharper preparation and more precise recommendations from day one.
