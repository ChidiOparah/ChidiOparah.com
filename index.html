The 6E Method™

From Chaos to Self-Correcting Execution

Chidi's proprietary six-stage framework — developed across 25 years of enterprise transformation — takes organisations from structural chaos to predictable, scalable delivery.

Stage 01
Evaluate

Rapid structural diagnosis across architecture, operating model, teams, and delivery flows to surface root causes — not symptoms.

Stage 02
Envision

Define the target state — a single, shared picture of how the business needs to run technically, organisationally, and strategically.

Stage 03
Empower

Rebuild leadership confidence and team ownership through clear governance, decision rights, and accountability structures.

Stage 04
Equip

Give teams the tools, processes, and architecture patterns needed to execute with consistency rather than firefighting.

Stage 05
Enable

Establish delivery rhythm, release predictability, and stakeholder visibility that shifts teams from reactive to forecasting mode.

Stage 06
Evolve

Embed continuous improvement loops so the organisation can self-correct, adapt, and sustain momentum without Chidi in the room.
